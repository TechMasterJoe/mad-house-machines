About Mad House Machines

Local partner for high-value Chinese CNC machines

Who We Are

Mad House Machines and Services is based in Corona, California. We specialize in sales and local service of MHM-branded manufacturing machines built by established Chinese CNC manufacturers.

Our two primary manufacturing partners are:

  • GreatCNC / Great Magtech (greatcncmachine.com) — vertical & horizontal machining centers, gantry mills, horizontal boring mills, vertical lathes, and high-speed centers.
  • JinsuCNC / Guangzhou Mingkong (jinsucnc.com) — high-speed engraving and milling machines, fully enclosed and desktop 3/4/5-axis systems.

We bridge the gap between competitive Chinese factory pricing and the local support Southern California shops actually need — installation help, training, and responsive service.
