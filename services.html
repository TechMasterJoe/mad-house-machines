Our Services

From machine selection to long-term local support

🛒

Machine Sales & Sourcing

We supply MHM-branded machines built by GreatCNC (Great Magtech) and JinsuCNC. Tell us your parts, materials, tolerances, and production volume — we’ll recommend the right model and configuration and provide a clear quote.

🚚

Delivery & Installation

Coordination of shipping, rigging guidance, and on-site installation support. We help you get the machine placed, leveled, and ready for production in your Corona-area facility.

🎓

Training & Start-Up

Operator and programming orientation so your team can run the machine confidently. Focused on practical shop use, not just theory.

🔧

Local Service & Support

Troubleshooting, preventive maintenance guidance, and local service response based in Corona, California. You’re not left waiting on overseas support for day-to-day issues.

📦

Spare Parts & Consumables

Assistance sourcing critical spare parts, tooling interfaces, and recommended consumables to keep your MHM machines running with minimal downtime.

📐

Application Consulting

Help matching machine travels, spindle, tool magazine, and options to your specific workpieces and production goals — before you buy.
